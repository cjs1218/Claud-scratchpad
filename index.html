GeneRoute

Your independent genomic test guide – tools for finding the right NHS genomic test.

Independent – not an NHS website or service

  • Cancer Family History Navigator

    Enter the cancers and histology in a family to see ranked germline cancer predisposition syndromes and who meets NHS Test Directory criteria.

    • Ranked syndromes, with the features behind each
    • Test Directory eligibility, Manchester score and bowel screening advice
    • Printable family summary

  • National Genomic Test Directory Explorer

    Search every rare and inherited disease test in the NHS National Genomic Test Directory by condition, gene or criteria wording.

    • Every R code, with its exact eligibility criteria
    • Genes on each test's PanelApp panel
    • Which tests include a given gene

In development. Clinical decision support for healthcare professionals only – not a substitute for clinical judgement, and the Test Directory always takes precedence. Content is AI-assisted and is being clinically reviewed; always check the linked sources. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere. Patients and relatives: please talk to your GP or genetics team rather than acting on these tools.

GeneRoute is independent: it is not an NHS website or service and is not provided or endorsed by the NHS or NHS England. It uses the publicly available NHS National Genomic Test Directory as a source.
